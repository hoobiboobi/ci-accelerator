{{- if and $vt.enabled $runner.enabled }}
The runner gives every job its own trace. With the port-forward above,
the trace of job <job> in pipeline <pipeline>:
  curl -s http://localhost:10428/select/jaeger/api/traces/01{{ $runner.gitlabUrl | sha256sum | trunc 6 }}$(printf '%012x%012x' <pipeline> <job>)
the jobs of pipeline <pipeline> and their trace ids:
  curl -s http://localhost:10428/select/logsql/query --data-urlencode start=7d \
    --data-urlencode 'query="resource_attr:ci.pipeline.id":"<pipeline>" | uniq by (trace_id, "resource_attr:ci.job.id")'
{{- end }}
Builds can reach this storage: they can read other projects' traces (the
buildctl command line is recorded, so never pass secrets as build args) and
change or delete data. Do not expose it outside the cluster.
{{- end }}

Builds share the daemons' cache: a build's RUN steps can read and write what
other builds left there. Use one installation per trust domain.
