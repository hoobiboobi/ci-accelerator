Build in a CI job (the job gets BUILDKIT_REMOTE_ADDR and the client
certificate in BUILDKIT_TLS_DIR):

  build:
    image: moby/buildkit:{{ .Chart.AppVersion }}
    script:
      - buildctl --addr "$BUILDKIT_REMOTE_ADDR" --tlsdir "$BUILDKIT_TLS_DIR"
          build --frontend dockerfile.v0 --local context=. --local dockerfile=.

Builds share the daemons' cache: a build's RUN steps can read and write what
other builds left there. Use one installation per trust domain.
