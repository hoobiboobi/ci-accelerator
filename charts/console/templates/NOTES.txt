The console reads the daemons behind {{ include "console.discover" . }}
{{- with include "console.jobsNamespace" . }} and the CI job pods in {{ . }}{{ end }}.

Its pages and API have no sign-in yet: keep it inside the cluster. To look
at it:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "console.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}

then open http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/ (pipelines, builds and why
they rebuilt, project insights, build nodes) or
http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/api/v1/builds?limit=10.
{{- if not (dig "token" "existingSecret" "" .Values.gitlab) }}

Job times come from the job pods. With a GitLab token that can read the
projects' jobs (gitlab.token.existingSecret), the pages also show the time
before each build (runner and checkout) and jobs that ran elsewhere.
{{- end }}

The store is on the PersistentVolumeClaim {{ include "console.pvcName" . }}
{{- if not .Values.persistence.existingClaim }}, which is kept when the release is removed{{ end }}.
